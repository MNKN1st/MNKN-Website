# MNKN — standalone story

I started touring in punk bands when I was thirteen. Before studios and screens, it was figuring out songs note by note. Music was instinctual to me from as far back as I can remember. I discovered early that I could best express myself through music. I went on to play shows all around the country from playing dive bars filled with middle aged men as a 15 year old kid with dyed hair to cutting my teeth across venues of all sizes across the country while developing as artists, working with producers, signing development deals. This was such a crucial point of my life, because it was in all of this where i found my love for the studio and the creation process.

By twenty, I was producing for up-and-coming UK grime artists in the North of England. That opened the door to throwing myself into the trap wave that burned through the EDM space as well as turning from local artist development to international hip-hop collaborations. My catalogue grew and I got to connect with some legends such as Timbaland, Kevin Gates, Professor Green, K Koke and continuously working with up and comers and landing some placements that added some real value to myself as a producer.

Alongside artist releases, my focus had found consistency in writing for tv, films, games and multimedia. For the last decade I have been creating a wide spread music catalogue that has manifested a whole base level of consistent placements around the world, I also found success in creating bespoke music for international super brands such as Adidas, JD Sports, McDonalds, Pepsi, Braun, Sports Direct, Everlast and McVities to name a few. As I have matured through this space I have been focused more and more on creating music for Trailers and a somewhat more developed and ambitious mindset.

I also have a passion for working towards a space of positive influence in the music creator and creator space in general. I have always documented my journey to some extent, and this is something that I love to do. It has allowed me to work with brands as well as connect with some interesting collaborators and clients, all through putting myself out there on Youtube and Social Media. I enjoy contributing to the space of information, thought and education from the experience that i have gathered over the last 2 decades.
